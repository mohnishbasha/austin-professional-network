# Community Guidelines

These are suggested principles for respectful, inclusive professional networking at Austin Bhet gatherings and in the online community. Organizers may adapt them, and each event can announce its own policies.

## Our commitments
1. **Every career stage belongs.** Students, job seekers, working professionals, career changers, and mentors deserve the same courtesy. Employment status, title, and seniority don't decide whose voice counts.
2. **Professional respect.** Disagree with ideas, not people. Discrimination, harassment, and disrespectful behavior are not tolerated.
3. **Authentic networking.** Have real conversations. Be honest about what you do and what you're looking for.
4. **Give before you ask.** Share what you know, make introductions, and offer feedback when asked.
5. **Mutual support.** Help peers through job searches and career changes. Celebrate their wins.
6. **Inclusion in the room.** Mix groups, invite quieter people into the conversation, and don't let the room split into separate clusters. Organizers can use icebreakers to help.

## Consent and privacy
- **Connections are mutual.** Ask before sending a LinkedIn request. Accept a "no" gracefully.
- **Photos.** Ask before photographing or recording anyone. If you'd rather not be photographed, say so, and others should respect that.
- **Contact details.** Don't collect, share, or reuse anyone's contact information without their permission. Never compile attendee lists.
- **What's shared in the room stays respectful.** Don't repost someone's personal story or job situation without their consent.

## Talks and sponsors
- Presentations are educational. They should not be sales pitches for the presenter's own business.
- Recruiters and hiring managers are welcome to describe roles. They should not pressure people or collect personal data without consent.
- Sponsors are acknowledged only with permission. Sponsorship does not include access to attendee information.

## Honest expectations
The community does not guarantee jobs, referrals, interviews, or other outcomes. Ask for referrals respectfully, and understand that people can say no.

## Raising a concern
Talk to an organizer at the event, or message a group admin in the [Amhi Austinkar LinkedIn Group](https://www.linkedin.com/groups/14014249). Organizers may ask anyone who breaks these guidelines to leave an event or the group.
